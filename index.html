Work Log Sync

Your work hours, logged without typing.

A Chrome extension that copies your work time and chat handle time into your personal work tracker, every minute, while you work.

Works with Zomans only, for now.

How to get started

  1. Open the trackerUse the button above and keep the tracker open in one browser tab. The extension writes your hours into it.
  2. Add the extension to ChromeInstall Work Log Sync from the Chrome Web Store link you were given, and pin it to your toolbar so the badge shows your hours today.
  3. Log in to ZomansStay logged in as usual. Your hours update on their own, and each closed chat adds its handle time.

The tracker

The extension works with a personal tracker web page. It shows your daily work time, average handle time, and history by day and month. You need the tracker open for the extension to update it.

Where it works

Right now the extension works only with Zomans (external.zomans.com). It reads your hours from your own logged-in Zomans session, so you need a Zomans account. Other websites are not supported.

What it does

  • Shows your work time for a 6 AM to 6 AM work day.
  • Checks for new hours every minute and updates your tracker.
  • Measures handle time for each chat, from when it lands to when you close it. This resets at midnight.
  • Lets you sync yesterday, this week, last week, this month, or last month by hand.
  • Follows your light or dark mode setting.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Work Log Sync copies your own work-time and chat handle-time data from Zomans into your own personal work tracker. This policy explains what it accesses and what happens to that data.

What the extension accesses

  • Your work-time data, read with your existing logged-in session.
  • Chat assigned and closed events seen while a Zomans page is open, used to calculate handle time. Chat IDs are turned into temporary tokens inside the page and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your tracker page, where the extension writes your work-time and handle-time values.

What is stored

A small amount of data stays on your device in Chrome extension storage: today's work-time totals, today's handle-time entries, sync status, and your settings. Handle-time entries are kept for the current day only.

What is not done

  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third-party server. There is no backend and no analytics.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • No remote code is loaded. All code ships inside the extension.

Your control

Turn off Auto-update in the extension popup, or remove the extension at any time. Removing it deletes its stored data.

Work Log Sync is an independent tool and is not an official product of Zomans or any employer.
